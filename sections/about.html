About RatNawn

Building practical AI for real businesses.

RatNawn Tech is an AI product and technology company based in Bengaluru, India. We build products and custom technology across Voice AI, Generative AI, intelligent automation, recruitment technology, creative AI and digital applications.

Our focus is simple: take emerging technology and turn it into systems businesses and customers can actually use. Some of our ideas become products such as Vocalis, Saral Rojgar and RatNawnAI. Others become custom platforms built around a specific business problem. Either way, our approach remains the same — understand the workflow, build quickly, put the technology into real use and continuously improve it.
